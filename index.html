Featured research · AI for science · 2026

CellCast ✳

Forecasting cellular responses before an experiment.

CellCast investigates how AI can predict changes in single-cell gene expression following perturbations. I worked on model implementation, training, debugging, and attention-architecture choices, balancing theoretical design with available computing resources.
